API COMPATIBILITY
GET /health and GET /forecast remain available. Existing start_date, end_date,
allow_historical and X-API-Key schemas and required forecast/row keys are retained.
Additional metric blocks and compare_variant_a diagnostics are additive. Service
identity and model_version intentionally identify an isolated 3.1 shadow.
Original frozen model_metadata is returned unchanged; complete scores are exposed
separately, with weekly retrospective scope. model_ready means loadable shadow
artifacts, not production approval. production_approved remains false.

Historical behavior is deliberately limited to saved origin parameters; no
arbitrary historical request may silently retune. Thirty/ninety-day responses
are structurally supported, but their accuracy was not evaluated by the saved
seven-day backtest. Bounds remain uncalibrated / Not evaluated.
